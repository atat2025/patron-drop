How it works

  1. Deposit USDC and mint SEI3X at the current NAV.
  2. SEI3X moves 3x the daily SEI move. SEI +1% → SEI3X ≈ +3%. SEI −1% → SEI3X ≈ −3%.
  3. Once a day the token rebalances back to 3x, so you never get liquidated.
  4. Redeem any time for USDC at NAV.

Risks

  • Losses are 3x too. A fast drop can wipe out most of the value.
  • Daily rebalancing causes decay in choppy markets. Not for long-term holding.
  • This is a testnet demo. Unaudited. Not an offer.
